# Research Design

## Business problem

Service organizations need to understand where clients encounter delay, drop-off, missed appointments, or repeated service needs.

## Primary analytical question

Where in the referral-to-service journey do clients experience the greatest operational friction, and how does that vary across sites, programs, and time?

## Secondary questions

1. What share of referrals complete intake?
2. What share of intake records reach a completed first service?
3. How long do clients wait between referral, intake, appointment, and service?
4. Which sites or programs miss service-level targets most often?
5. Where are no-show and cancellation rates highest?
6. Which cohorts are most likely to return for additional service?
7. Are operational outcomes improving or deteriorating over time?

## Unit of analysis

The project uses multiple related units:

- client
- referral
- case
- appointment
- service event
- site/program/month

## Responsible interpretation

All data are synthetic. The project evaluates operational performance, not client eligibility or protected-class risk.
